Dokubee Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

NosWorks ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Dokubee mobile game ("Dokubee" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Dokubee.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Dokubee's privacy practices, you can contact us at:

dev@johnson-le.com

Information Stored by Dokubee

Dokubee stores certain game information locally on your device so that the game can function properly. This may include:

  • Game progress
  • Completed and current levels
  • Game settings
  • Tutorial progress
  • Audio and haptic preferences
  • Other gameplay state

This locally stored gameplay information is used to provide the game's functionality.

NosWorks does not require you to create an account or sign in to play Dokubee.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Dokubee is supported by advertising.

The App uses Unity services, including Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads, to provide and manage advertisements such as banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

These advertising services may automatically collect or receive certain information when you use Dokubee or interact with advertisements.

Depending on the service and how it is used, this information may include:

  • User identifiers
  • Purchase history
  • Approximate location
  • Advertising and app interaction information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Device identifiers and other device-related identifiers

This information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing app and advertising functionality
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements
  • Analytics
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security and compliance

Information may be shared with Unity and its advertising services as necessary to provide these services.

Dokubee currently uses Unity LevelPlay mediation, the ironSource Exchange, and Unity Ads. Additional advertising partners may be added in future versions of the App. If our data practices materially change, this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures will be updated accordingly.

For more information about how Unity processes information, please review Unity's privacy policies and documentation:

https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Information We Do Not Directly Request

Dokubee does not require users to provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Audio recordings
  • Health or fitness information
  • Financial account or payment-card information

Dokubee does not provide user accounts or an account login system.

Third-party advertising services may collect the limited categories of information described in the Advertising and Third-Party Services section above.

How Information Is Used

Information processed through the advertising services used by Dokubee may be used for purposes including:

  • App functionality
  • Advertising and marketing
  • Analytics
  • Advertising measurement
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Legal and regulatory compliance

NosWorks uses locally stored gameplay information to operate Dokubee and preserve your game progress and preferences.

Data Sharing

Dokubee may share information with third-party service providers that are necessary to provide advertising and related services.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

NosWorks does not sell information that users directly provide to us.

Data Security

Information transmitted by the third-party services used by Dokubee is transmitted using secure connections and encryption in transit where provided by those services.

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Dokubee. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and settings stored locally by Dokubee remain on your device until they are changed, reset, removed by the App, or the App's local data is deleted.

Information processed by Unity and advertising services may be retained according to those providers' respective privacy and data-retention practices.

For questions or requests concerning privacy or data associated with Dokubee, contact:

dev@johnson-le.com

We will evaluate privacy and deletion requests and, where applicable, work with our service providers to address them.

Children's Privacy

Dokubee is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

Our advertising and data-handling practices may vary depending on applicable laws, the user's region, and the age groups for which Dokubee is made available.

The App's target audience and advertising configuration are established in accordance with applicable platform and advertising requirements.

International Users

Information processed by third-party service providers may be transferred to and processed in countries other than the country in which you live.

Those providers are responsible for handling such information according to their privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Dokubee changes, including when features, advertising providers, or data practices change.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
